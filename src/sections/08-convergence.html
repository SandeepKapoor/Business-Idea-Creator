The second diamond — the step almost everyone skips

Once one direction survives, re-diverge on how to deliver it — 20 format options × 20 pricing options — then converge again. People run the first diamond, pick a winner, and then default to "so I'll make a course" out of habit. That's exactly how a genuinely good idea becomes a mediocre business.
