9 · The 2-week validation sprint

Money is the only signal that isn't a lie.

Interview rule
Ask about the past, never the future
Banned: "would you pay for this?" — everyone says yes and it means nothing. Ask instead: "What's the last thing you paid for to get better at design, how much, and did you finish it?" · "Walk me through the last time you tried to hire a designer and it went badly." · "What did you spend your training budget on last year?" Past behaviour with a price attached is the only real data.
The money test
Pre-sell before you build
Take real payment for a thing that doesn't exist yet, with a written promise to refund. Not a waitlist, not an interest form, not a "sign up for early access" — those all measure politeness. A signal ladder: reply < call < intro < deposit < full payment. Only the last two count.
Pre-committed kill criteria
Write these down now, before you start
Kill it if, in 14 days: fewer than 12 of 20 interviews describe the problem unprompted · zero people pay before it exists · you can't reach the buyer without paid ads · or delivery needs someone you haven't hired. Decide the numbers today, while you still have no ego invested in the answer.
