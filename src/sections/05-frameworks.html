3 · The frameworks

15 generators. The three doing most of the work are marked.

Divergence trick 1
Quantity gate before quality gate
100 ideas before judging any. Ideas 4–20 are clichés — they're the ones you already listed. The good ones live at 60–100, because only by then have you exhausted the obvious.
Divergence trick 2
Deliberate bad-idea round
Generate 20 terrible ones on purpose (cluster J below). It kills self-censorship, and two of them are always secretly good. #104 in this bank came out of that round.
Divergence trick 3
Specificity forcing
"A design course" is not an idea. If you can't name the person and the outcome, it's a placeholder. Every one of the 114 below names both — hold yourself to that.
