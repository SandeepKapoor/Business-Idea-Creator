# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

One user: Sandeep, a designer with a background at Boeing and Politecnico di Milano, deciding
which business to start in design education. He opens the file alone, on a laptop, usually while
already thinking about something specific — not browsing.

Confirmed: nobody else opens it. It is a private thinking tool, not a portfolio piece, not a
document shown to clients. Design serves speed of use and density of information; it has no one
to persuade.

## Product Purpose

Turn an unbounded question — "what business should I start?" — into a bounded one he can act on.
It holds 114 researched ideas, a rules engine that scores any of 193,600 four-axis combinations,
a watch list of emerging niches, and an analysis of how 23 Indian providers actually make money.

Success is a decision he can defend to himself in three months, plus the reasoning that produced
it. Not comprehensiveness. The document already contains far more than he will use; its job is to
make the useful part reachable.

Confirmed 2026-08-05: **this is a living instrument, not a document that retires.** Reaching the
decision does not finish it. He keeps running the generator afterwards, the Frontier decays and
gets refilled, verified figures replace judgment calls, and the analyser gains markets. Design for
something that stays current — not for a deliverable that gets handed over and stops.

## Positioning

Every number carries its provenance. A neighbouring tool could copy the idea bank; it could not
copy the discipline that every price shows where it came from, every score says whether it was
measured or judged, and the document names its own weakest claims in an evidence ledger.

## Operating Context

- Opened from a local file or `localhost:3007`, in Chrome, on a desktop-class screen.
- Used in short sessions: change one axis, read the result, change another. Not read end to end.
- Four tabs, and only ever one visible: **the full report** (13 sections, an argued document),
  **idea workspace** (pick four axes, get a scored idea and a business case), **the frontier**
  (10 sourced windows), **business models** (10 models across 23 providers).
- Light and dark themes, both in daily use.
- The whole thing is regenerated by `node build.js`, which concatenates `src/` into one file.
- Also pushed to `github.com/SandeepKapoor/Business-Idea-Creator` and served by GitHub Pages.
  Confirmed 2026-08-05: **publishing is hosting convenience, not an audience.** It is how he
  reaches the file from anywhere. It does not make the artifact public-facing, and it does not
  justify generalising the copy, softening the second-person address, or adding onboarding for
  strangers arriving cold.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Absolute: one self-contained HTML file.** No external stylesheet, script, font, image or
  `@import`. Exactly one `<style>` and one `<script>`. Confirmed still binding, which means
  **system fonts only** — the visual system works through scale, spacing, colour and layout.
- Zero dependencies, zero build tooling beyond plain Node. No framework, no bundler.
- Concatenation, not bundling: one shared script scope, inline `onclick` handlers.
- Source order is load-bearing; files carry two-digit prefixes.
- Five harnesses gate every change: `verify` (160), `fold` (44), `picker` (32), `design` (22),
  `probe` (43, measured in real Chrome at 1280, 760 and 500 — `npm run check` runs all three
  widths). A visual change that breaks an epistemic check is a failed change, not a trade-off.
- Content and numbers are frozen for this pass. Ideas, scores, sources and provenance chips are
  not to be rewritten as a side effect of a design pass.
- The four-tab structure is frozen. Layout, navigation and hierarchy inside a tab are open.
- **Density is a requirement, not a defect.** It must not be thinned to look calmer.
- Deliberately undecided: the analyser covers India only. UK and USA come once the India material
  has been read and corrected. Do not build them speculatively.

**Two unresolved factual defects**, both carried openly rather than quietly fixed:

- The evidence ledger says nine of twenty-two formats have a researched base price. `BASE_EV`
  flags eight, and the `[R]` markers in the pricing comment agree with `BASE_EV`. The safe
  correction is the ledger — flipping a `BASE_EV` flag would assert evidence that does not exist,
  which is the exact failure mode this artifact is built to prevent. `verify` reports it every run.
- `CNAME` contains `sandeepkapoor.github.io.ideas`, which is not a valid domain. GitHub Pages
  cannot serve a custom domain on that value.

## Brand Commitments

Name: *Business Idea Map*. No logo, no external identity, nothing binding to anyone else.

The visual world is **Vermillion Broadsheet**, chosen 6 Aug 2026 and recorded in full in the
contract at the top of `src/index.html`. Three values and no fourth: warm ink is the record,
vermillion is anything live or selected, and one cold slate is reserved for what failed. The
accent marks content, never chrome — a live region takes a hairline box with a single vermillion
rule down its left edge, never a four-sided accent outline.

## Evidence on Hand

- 114 ideas across 12 clusters, 8 of them hand-scored, the rest derived.
- Researched price anchors for 8 of 22 delivery formats; the other 14 are marked as unanchored.
- 81 route descriptions, 7 kinds of work, 7 twists, all derived from the bank.
- Frontier: 10 entries carrying 13 sources, 7 of them primary or named research, 2 content
  marketing. Business models: 23 providers across 10 models, 19 sources, 11 primary or named.
- **Absences that must not be filled by invention:** no market size anywhere, no competitor
  revenue, margin or enrolment, no customer validation of any idea. Three of the eight scoring
  criteria are explicitly guesses about Sandeep rather than about the market.

## Product Principles

1. **Provenance travels with the number.** A figure without its source is a defect, not a detail.
2. **Say which kind of claim it is** — counted, published, or judged — and never blend them.
3. **Reachability over completeness.** The document is already too big; the work is making the
   right part findable in seconds.
4. **Density is the product.** Comparison needs things side by side. Do not buy calm with scrolling.
5. **A check, not a promise.** Every guarantee above is enforced by a harness that has been proved
   able to fail.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Single known user, no assistive-technology requirement stated. The project nonetheless holds
itself to: WCAG AA contrast in both themes (verified by measurement), visible focus rings on every
interactive control, no meaning carried by colour alone, keyboard operation for the axis picker
and every disclosure, and `prefers-reduced-motion` respected. These are existing commitments with
existing checks; the revamp must not lower them.
